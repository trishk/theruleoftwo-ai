# TheRuleOfTwo.ai

## Vision

TheRuleOfTwo.ai is a group chat where people invite AI perspectives to make better decisions together.

The application should feel as natural as WhatsApp, while allowing AI participants to join conversations.

---

# Problem

Today's AI chat applications are built around one user talking to one AI.

Real decisions are collaborative.

People discuss important decisions with partners, friends, colleagues and advisors.

TheRuleOfTwo extends that idea by allowing AI experts to participate naturally in those conversations.

---

# MVP

## Personal MVP

One user.

Create and manage chats.

Send messages.

Mention AI participants.

Receive AI responses.

Conversation persists.

Multiple AI providers.

---

## Collaborative MVP

Google authentication.

Invite another person to a chat.

Shared conversation.

Owner and member roles.

Multiple AI participants.

Bring Your Own API Keys.

Conversation-aware AI responses.

Transparent AI usage costs.

---

# Future

Memory.

Voice.

Mobile.

Additional authentication providers.

Advanced usage analytics.

---

# Principles

Conversation first.

AI second.

Simple before powerful.

Fast before perfect.

Provider agnostic.

Everything should feel like messaging.

---

# Non-goals

Not another ChatGPT wrapper.

Not an AI benchmark website.

Not a prompt engineering tool.

Not a coding assistant.

The product is about collaborative decision making.
